# Clara

Clara is an open-source, AI-powered personal assistant that helps users manage everyday work and life through conversation.

## Project Vision

Clara connects to the services users rely on and helps them get things done with their permission. Connected services must only be accessed after the user grants permission.

## Planned Platforms

- Android mobile app (first planned platform)
- Windows desktop app
- iOS app
- macOS app

## Skills

Clara is being developed with skills for:

- Email assistance
- Presentation creation
- Research assistance
- Coding assistance
- Task and reminder management

## Integrations

Planned integrations include Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, and Google Tasks, with more services to follow.

## Voice and Memory

Continuous microphone access is optional. When enabled, Clara may use daily voice context to suggest forgotten tasks, reminders, or follow-ups. Voice access must remain opt-in and privacy-conscious.

## Language Models

Users provide their own API keys for the language-model providers they choose, including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google Gemini.

## Contribution and Status

Clara is actively developed as a large open-source project. Features, integrations, and supported platforms will evolve over time. Contributors should read `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
