# Code of Conduct

This project is committed to providing a respectful, professional, and inclusive environment for everyone who participates in its development and community.

The project is maintained by Aryan Vishwakarma, and all contributors, reviewers, and participants are expected to uphold the standards described in this document.

## Our standards

We expect all participants to:

- be respectful, courteous, and constructive in communication
- engage in good faith and with professionalism
- give and receive feedback thoughtfully and without personal hostility
- focus on the merits of ideas, proposals, and contributions
- be inclusive and welcoming to contributors from different backgrounds and experience levels

We do not tolerate:

- harassment, intimidation, or abusive behavior
- discrimination or exclusionary conduct
- personal attacks, insults, or demeaning language
- threatening behavior or retaliatory conduct
- disruption of discussions, reviews, or collaborative work

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories and discussions
- issue reports and pull request interactions
- communication channels used for project collaboration
- in-person or virtual meetings associated with the project

## Reporting concerns

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it promptly.

Please contact the project owner directly:

- Email: aryanvishwakarma275@gmail.com
- GitHub: Aryan-202

When reporting, include as much detail as possible, including:

- what happened
- when and where it occurred
- who was involved
- any supporting context or evidence

Reports will be reviewed promptly, treated confidentially, and handled with seriousness and discretion.

## Enforcement

The project owner is responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct and for deciding on appropriate responses to reported incidents.

Possible responses may include:

- a private warning
- a request to stop or modify behavior
- removal from participation in project spaces
- rejection of contributions that violate the standards of the project

Enforcement decisions will be made in a fair and consistent manner, with attention to the facts and context of each report.

## Appeals

If you believe a decision affecting your participation has been handled unfairly, you may contact the project owner to request a review of the matter.

## Commitment

We believe that a healthy and respectful community is essential to building reliable software and maintaining trust among contributors. This Code of Conduct exists to protect that standard and to support constructive collaboration.

We thank everyone who contributes to making this project a professional and welcoming space.
