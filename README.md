# Clara

Clara is an AI-powered personal assistant designed to help you manage your everyday work and life through a simple conversation.

Just chat with Clara and ask her to help. She can assist with tasks such as email, calendars, documents, research, presentations, and coding.

## Vision

Clara is being built as an open-source personal assistant that connects with you personally and helps you get things done. With your permission, Clara can connect to the services you use every day.

## Planned Platforms

The Android mobile app is the first platform planned for Clara.

Coming soon:

- Windows desktop app
- iOS app
- macOS app

## Skills

Clara is being developed with a growing set of skills, including:

- Email assistance
- Presentation creation
- Research assistance
- Coding assistance
- Task and reminder management

## Integrations

With your permission, Clara will be able to work with services such as:

- Gmail
- Google Calendar
- Google Drive
- Google Tasks
- And many more

Clara will only access connected services when you grant permission.

## Voice and Memory

If you choose to give Clara continuous microphone access, she may listen for useful context throughout your day. At the end of the day, Clara could suggest tasks, reminders, or follow-ups based on things you mentioned and may have forgotten.

Voice access will always be optional and should be enabled only when you are comfortable with it.

## Language Models

During the initial stages, Clara will use API keys from different large language model providers, including:

- OpenAI
- Anthropic
- Google Gemini
- Other providers as the project grows

You will provide your own API key for the providers you want to use.
